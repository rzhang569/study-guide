Midterm review: from counting to expectation

A demo page built by the study-guide Agent Skill. PROB 101 is a fictional course; nothing here comes from a real class.

Organized by lecture. Every claim carries a slide reference such as L2 p.4, so you can check it against the deck. Each lecture opens with a short glossary; learn the words first, then read on.

When
Week 6, in class, 30 minutes
Format
True/false, multiple choice, fill-in, two short answers
Allowed
One handwritten note sheet
Not allowed
Calculator, phone, AI. Keep the numbers easy to work by hand
Scope
L1–L3
Weight
20% of the course grade

How to use this page

  • Tags: core on the practice sheet or worked on the slides; likely covered in class and easy to turn into a true/false; background read once.
  • Each topic: read the one-line lead after the green bar, then the figure, then the details and the red Trap boxes. Dashed In plain words boxes explain the idea without notation.
  • Practice: every lecture ends with a practice box, so you can test yourself right after reading. At the bottom: the full practice bank, step-by-step short answers, timed mock exams drawn from their own pool, and a mistake notebook that fills itself.
  • Option text can be selected with the mouse; a drag that selects text does not count as a click.
